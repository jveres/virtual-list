virtual-list — lean-core-v2 branch

The v2 rewrite (see docs/lean-core.md), built on two preserved primitives: the browser-adaptive scroll-settle controller and detached premeasurement.

  • scroll-settle lab — verify the settle signal and settle-time structural commits in every browser
  • dynamic-height lab — adversarial size-changing content on the v2 runtime; the panel reports contract violations observed from outside the runtime
  • streaming chat — the acceptance target, hosted on the v2 runtime in live mode
  • gallery — justified rows or masonry bands as items: exact geometry before any image loads, re-flows anchored through the runtime, paging at the end
  • feed — Bluesky Discover and Lemmy, measured posts and images, bounded paging, and fresh-head loading
