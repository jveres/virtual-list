virtual-list

A virtualizer (see docs/core-contract.md), built on two primitives: the browser-adaptive scroll-settle controller and in-canvas measurement of rows in hidden slots.

  • streaming chat — the acceptance target, hosted on the runtime
  • gallery — justified rows or whole-photo masonry items: exact geometry before any image loads, re-flows anchored through the runtime, paging at the end
  • feed — Bluesky Discover and Lemmy, measured posts and images, bounded paging, and fresh-head loading
  • newspaper — four editorial layouts with premeasured blocks and viewport-prioritized images
  • chat history — 10,000 numbered messages, bounded two-way paging, and direct jumps that load a page around any message
  • dynamic-height lab — adversarial size-changing content on the runtime; the panel reports contract violations observed from outside the runtime
  • scroll-settle lab — verify the settle signal and settle-time structural commits in every browser
